## The Query
> "I fell for a mortal who loves garlic bread. Do I tell her the truth or just keep wearing a mask to dinner?"

## LoveBites' Verdict
My dear nocturnal friend, deception is a fragile foundation for any romance, especially one involving high-carb Italian appetizers. You cannot spend eternity in a respirator. Tell her the truth. If she truly loves you, she'll switch to pesto. If not, well, there are plenty of other necks in the sea. And please, do check your cape for crumbs before you leave.
